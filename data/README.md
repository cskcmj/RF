The 44 oxygen and 35 water-vapour spectroscopic rows are transcribed from
ITU-R P.676. The numeric P.676-12 tables in ITU-Rpy match Tables 1 and 2 in
P.676-13 Annex 1. The model in rf_atmosphere.py uses P.676-13 equations 1–9.

Downloaded on 2026-11-04 from the ITU-Rpy project by Inigo del Portillo:

- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/inigodelportillo/ITU-Rpy/master/itur/data/676/v12_lines_water_vapour.txt
